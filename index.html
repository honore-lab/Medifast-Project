About MediFast

MediFast was designed to bridge the critical gap between emergency events and medical response. By optimizing patient intake and coordination, we ensure that every second counts when it matters most.
Our goal is to eliminate systemic delays in medical data transfer, providing responders with accurate, real-time information to improve patient outcomes.

Our Mission

My mission is to make healthcare better focusing on making healthcare work efficient and emergency medical services in Africa better. Many hospitals and healthcare centers have patient waiting times and delays in emergency medical response. This makes healthcare services less effective. Sometimes puts patients lives at risk. These challenges are common in African healthcare systems where there are too many patients, poor patient scheduling and inefficient communication.
